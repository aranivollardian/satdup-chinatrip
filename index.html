北京

Beijing

Sun 27 to Thu 31 Dec
4nights
Beijing on the mapYour hotel: Qianmen

You're in a hutong just off Qianmen Street, south of Tiananmen Square. Tiananmen is about 1.3 km on foot, the Temple of Heaven is close, and Zhushikou metro is 640 m away.

Sun 27 DecLand, settle, frozen lakes

Land at 10am, private car to the hotel (about 45 minutes). Nap.

Late afternoon at Shichahai. The frozen lake turns into a rink with ice chairs and sleds once the ice is thick enough, usually from late December. Wander the hutong lanes around Yandai Xiejie after.

Adya: the ice chairs are the highlight of Beijing for most small kids.

Mon 28 DecWalk the Great Wall at Mutianyu

Driver collects you at 8:30am, about 90 minutes each way. Your ticket is a round-trip cable car to Tower 14. Walk east along the wall towards Tower 6 and back, as far as Adya manages; it's restored watchtowers and big valley views the whole way. If you'd rather finish at Tower 6, buy a chairlift ticket down there on the day and tell the driver.

Don't head west from Tower 14. It gets very steep, and there may be ice. Bring the carrier for Adya.

Why Monday: the Forbidden City is closed.

Tue 29 DecTiananmen Square, Forbidden City, Jingshan

Driver and English-speaking guide from 8:30am. Tiananmen Square first, then north through Tiananmen Gate straight into the Forbidden City. Allow three hours inside and bring the carrier; it's a long walk on stone.

Climb Jingshan Park behind the north gate for the view over the golden roofs at sunset. Peking duck for dinner.

Wed 30 DecTemple of Heaven, Summer Palace

Driver and guide from 8:30am. Temple of Heaven first, while locals are doing tai chi, dancing and hacky sack in the park.

Afternoon at the Summer Palace. Kunming Lake freezes over in winter and turns into a huge skating and ice-chair area in front of the palace.

Pingyao train tomorrow at 10:38 from Beijing Fengtai, about 25 minutes by car from the hotel.

Tours and guides

Booked: private car and driver 28 to 30 Dec (driver speaks Mandarin), plus an English-speaking guide on 29 and 30 Dec. Great Wall day is driver only, so you walk at your own pace.

Where you're staying

Tian'anmen Front Street Left Court HotelBooked

Family Friendly Family Room, 27 to 31 December. A 21-room boutique hotel in a hutong off Qianmen Street, each room designed differently, with afternoon tea and a free minibar. No pool or spa, so plan warm-ups in cafés and the room.

平遥

Pingyao

Thu 31 Dec to Sat 2 Jan
2nights
Pingyao on the mapStay: inside the walls

Stay inside the walls, in a restored courtyard house. The old town is mostly car-free, so you walk everywhere.

Thu 31 DecBullet train west, New Year's Eve inside the walls

Train G625 from Beijing Fengtai at 10:38 (leave the hotel by 9:30), arriving Pingyao Gucheng about 14:10. Xihe collects you from the station.

Afternoon wandering Ming-Qing Street and the old lanes. Rishengchang, China's first draft bank, shows how Pingyao's merchants ran money across the empire.

New Year's Eve under the red lanterns. Quiet, local and nothing like a big-city countdown.

Fri 1 JanCity wall, courtyard mansions

Morning walk on the 14th-century city wall, then the old temples and yamen (magistrate's court) inside town.

Afternoon with a hotel-arranged driver to the Wang Family Courtyard or Qiao Family Courtyard, vast merchant compounds about 45 to 60 minutes away. Or keep it short: Shuanglin Temple and its painted clay sculptures, 15 minutes by taxi.

Adya: courtyards are made for running around in.

Exploring on your own

Inside the walls, no guide needed. The old town is compact, flat and mostly car-free. Walking the streets is free; one combined ticket covers the city wall, Rishengchang bank, the magistrate's court and the other sites for your whole stay. Buy it at any site or ask Xihe. Electric carts run around town when Adya's legs give out.

Do a little homework. Read up on Pingyao as China's old banking capital before you go, or use the audio guide at Rishengchang. That covers most of what a guide would tell you.

Outside the walls, just a driver. Shuanglin Temple is about 15 minutes away by taxi or Didi. For the Wang or Qiao Family Courtyard, ask Xihe to arrange a car and driver for Friday afternoon.

Where you're staying

Xihe Academy MansionBooked

Shuyuan Ondol Room, 31 December to 2 January. A restored scholars' academy inside the walls. The ondol room has a heated floor, which is ideal at minus 10°C. Ask them to arrange your station pickup and a driver for Friday afternoon.

Trip.com about 9.8 from 1,000+ reviews. Hotel page
西安

Xi'an

Sat 2 to Mon 4 Jan
2nights
Xi'an on the mapYour hotel: West Street

You're inside the city walls on West Street, about a kilometre from the Drum Tower, the Muslim Quarter and the wall itself. Qiaozikou metro is next door.

Sat 2 JanBullet train south, Muslim Quarter

Train D2799 from Pingyao Gucheng at 11:36, arriving Xi'an North about 14:03. A last slow morning in Pingyao first.

Evening: Muslim Quarter food streets and the Bell and Drum Towers lit up.

Sun 3 JanTerracotta Army, Huaqing Palace, City Wall

Booked private tour with an English-speaking guide, 8am pickup from the hotel, about 9 hours. Terracotta Army first, early, before the holiday crowds. Then Huaqing Palace, the Tang emperors' hot-spring palace, about 15 minutes away. Back into town for the City Wall in the late afternoon.

Adya: it's a long day. Pack snacks and the carrier, and let her nap in the car between stops.

Mon 4 JanDumpling class, bullet train to Chongqing

Morning home visit and cooking class, hand-pulled noodles or dumplings. Kept from your agent's plan because it's good.

Train G2207 from Xi'an North at 12:51, arriving Chongqing West about 18:11. Keep the cooking class to the early morning and leave for the station by 11:45; it's about 40 minutes. Check in at the Vista and head out for dinner.

Tours and guides

Booked: private day tour on Sun 3 Jan (Terracotta Army, Huaqing Palace, City Wall) with an English-speaking guide, a business 7-seater with driver and a child car seat, 8am pickup. Everything else in Xi'an is walkable from the hotel.

Where you're staying

Guardian Han TangBooked

Glorious Tang Dynasty Two-Bedroom Family Suite, 2 to 4 January. Every room is a suite with a living room and two bathrooms, designed around Han and Tang dynasty style, with a museum exhibition in the lobby. Spa at extra cost. It opened in 2026, so check recent reviews closer to the date.

重庆

Chongqing

Mon 4 to Wed 6 Jan, fly out 8:30pm
2nights
Chongqing on the mapYour hotel: Jiefangbei

You're on the 45th floor of a tower on Xinhua Road in Jiefangbei, walking distance to Hongyadong, the Yangtze cableway and Bayi Road food street. No breakfast at the hotel, so eat on Bayi Road.

Tue 5 JanPandas, 3D city, Nanshan by night

Morning at Chongqing Zoo for the giant pandas, at opening when they're most active. Then Liziba, where the monorail runs straight through an apartment block, and Eling Park's viewing tower. Lunch at Eling Second Factory.

Rest at the hotel in the afternoon.

Early hot pot dinner (order the split yuanyang pot, one side spicy, one side mild), then a taxi up Nanshan, about 30 minutes, to the One Tree viewing platform. The whole peninsula and both rivers lit up below you is the classic Chongqing night view.

Adya: aim to be up there by 7pm and back by 9. Bring a warm layer; it's breezy on the mountain.

Wed 6 JanCableway, Jiangtan Park, Chongqing 1949, evening flight

Check out and leave bags at the hotel. Early, take a Didi to the south station of the Yangtze River Cableway (Shangxin Street) and ride north across the river. The queue at the south end is usually much shorter.

Walk to Hongyadong, then across Qiansimen Bridge to Jiangtan Park for the view back across the river. Early lunch.

2pm: Chongqing 1949 at its purpose-built theatre in Ciqikou, about 30 minutes by Didi. The audience sits on a 360° rotating platform that moves with the stage, about 75 minutes, no interval. Book Zone A or B, the rotating seats. It's in Chinese, so rent the English translation headphones; arrive 30 minutes early.

Afterwards, a wander through Ciqikou old town next door, then back to the hotel for bags and an early dinner. Leave for the airport by 6:30pm. Flight 3U8011 departs 8:30pm from Jiangbei T3 and lands in Lijiang at 10:00pm.

Adya: under 1.2 m she goes free without a seat, but 75 minutes on a lap is long, so consider buying her a seat. The story is a wartime prison drama with loud effects, so sit by an aisle in case you need a break. Pyjamas on for the flight.

Tours and guides

Trip.com: Chongqing day tours

The top-rated city tour (cableway, Liziba monorail, Hongyadong) is a Trip.Select small group with thousands of reviews, and kids go free. Filter to private if you'd rather go alone, and ask to add the zoo in the morning.

Where you're staying

Vista High-Rise River View HotelBooked

Dream of Hidden River 270° River-View Suite, 4 to 6 January: 85 m² on the 46th floor, with afternoon tea and oil tea included. Lobby on the 45th floor, with curved floor-to-ceiling windows over the confluence, Qiansimen Bridge and Hongyadong. Smart rooms with a projector and a window bathtub, plus 24-hour butlers. Luggage desk on the ground floor. No pool or breakfast; they make Chongqing oil tea in the mornings.

丽江

Lijiang

Arrive 10pm Wed 6 Jan, leave Sun 10 Jan
4nights
Lijiang on the mapYour hotel: by the Old TownTiger Leaping Gorge

One base for all four nights, a 10-minute walk from Sifang Street in the Old Town, so dinners are on foot. Jade Dragon is about 50 minutes' drive. You're at 2,400 m, so pace the first day.

Thu 7 JanShuhe, Baisha, a Naxi home

Sleep in after the late arrival. Easy day while you adjust to the altitude. Shuhe Ancient Town in the morning. Baisha village and its murals, then a Naxi family home visit with fresh soybean milk.

Optional: an evening wander through Lijiang Old Town once the day crowds thin out.

Fri 8 JanJade Dragon Snow Mountain

Early start, about an hour's drive. Spruce Meadow cable car (3,240 m) and Blue Moon Valley's turquoise pools.

Adya: skip the Glacier Park cable car. 4,506 m is serious altitude for a three-year-old, and Spruce Meadow still gives you the snow peak up close.

The cable cars close in high wind or snow. If Friday's forecast is bad, swap this day with Saturday.

Sat 9 JanTiger Leaping Gorge

Day trip with a private driver, about two hours each way. At the Upper Gorge, a long stone staircase takes you down to a platform right beside the rapids, with the gorge walls towering above.

Adya: carrier on the stairs. There's a paid escalator for part of the climb back up.

Sun 10 JanFly to Shanghai

Leave the hotel by 9:30am. Flight MU6146 departs 11:40am and lands at Shanghai Pudong T1 at 3:00pm.

Tours and guides

Private Jade Dragon and Baisha day tour

Private guide and car. It's customisable: ask them to do Spruce Meadow and Blue Moon Valley rather than the Glacier Park cable car.

Private Tiger Leaping Gorge day trip, Yunnan Adventure Travel

Trip.com's gorge listings have very few reviews, so this one is on Tripadvisor. It's a long-running local operator with strong reviews. Alternatively your hotel can arrange a car and driver.

Where you're staying

InterContinental Lijiang Ancient Town ResortBooked

King Junior Suite, 6 to 10 January (non-refundable). Naxi courtyard architecture with Jade Dragon views. Heated indoor pool (13 m by 25 m) with a children's area, plus sauna and steam room. Restaurants on site for the nights after Jade Dragon and the gorge; the Old Town for the others. Bring the carrier: the lanes are cobbled.

上海

Shanghai

Sun 10 to Wed 13 Jan, fly out that night
3nights
Shanghai on the mapYour hotel: XintiandiHuanghe Road dumplings

You're by Xintiandi's restored shikumen lanes, a short walk to the French Concession and Tianzifang. The Bund, Yu Garden and Huanghe Road's famous dumpling shops (Jia Jia Tang Bao, Yang's Fried Dumplings) are a short taxi or metro ride.

Sun 10 JanArrive, Bund lights

Land at Pudong T1 at 3:00pm. Allow about an hour to the city by car. Evening walk on the Bund waterfront as the Pudong towers light up.

Mon 11 JanYu Garden, the Old City, Shanghai Tower

Yu Garden at opening, then the lanes and breakfast stalls of the Old City around it, the most local part of central Shanghai.

Shanghai Tower observation deck at sunset for the view over the whole city.

Tue 12 JanWater town, French Concession lanes

Morning at Zhujiajiao, a canal town with stone bridges and boats, about an hour west. Go early, before the day-trippers.

Afternoon in the plane-tree lanes of the French Concession around Wukang Road. Coffee, courtyards, no agenda.

Wed 13 JanDisneyland, then home

Check out, bags in a private car for the day. Shanghai Disneyland on a January weekday means short queues, and it's about 25 minutes from Pudong airport. Head to the airport 3 hours before your flight.

Adya: the reward for 18 days of good travelling.

Where you're staying

The Langham Shanghai, XintiandiBooked

10 to 13 January. Indoor pool in the Chuan Spa, with Xintiandi's lane-house restaurants on the doorstep. Free cancellation until 6pm on 9 January. On the last day, leave bags with the private car for Disney and the airport.

Warm clothes shopping list

Coldest days are around minus 10°C in Beijing and Pingyao, plus a snowy day on Jade Dragon. A waterproof long down jacket does the job of a shell and a down jacket, and adults don't need overpants: merino thermals under normal trousers are enough. Your ticks are saved on this device.

Winter kit for Adya

  • Beijing and Pingyao drop to minus 10°C: thermals, a proper puffer, hat and mittens on a string
  • Stick-on heat packs for pockets on Great Wall day
  • A carrier over a stroller: the Wall, the Forbidden City and the gorge are all steps
  • Lighter layers for Chongqing and Shanghai

Apps and money

  • Alipay and WeChat Pay run everything, taxis included
  • Didi works inside Alipay
  • Amap or Apple Maps, not Google Maps
  • Five-star hotels take cards; street food won't
